About Goat Village

Elite Livestock Breeding for Commercial Agriculture

Goat Village Zambia is a premium goat breeding and livestock investment farm located in Kamaila, Chisamba Lusaka. The farm focuses on superior Boer Goats and Red Kalahari Goats known for exceptional growth rates, hardiness, fertility, and meat quality.

Our operations are designed around modern livestock management practices, selective breeding programs, investor-ready scalability, and sustainable herd development for commercial agriculture markets.

Boer Goats

Fast-growing meat goats originally from South Africa with superior muscle development and breeding value.

Red Kalahari

Highly resilient goats with strong heat tolerance and excellent adaptability for African conditions.
